---
name: Goal color identity
description: Goal colors identify individual goals, not their shared life area.
---

Each goal needs its own consistent color across the application, including goals in the same area.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a unique color for each goal. Color should help recognize the same goal across planning and time reporting.

**How to apply:** Preserve assigned goal colors through renames, area/status changes, sorting, refreshes, and account saves. Assign unused colors to new or legacy goals; do not reuse one area's color as every goal's identity. Keep semantic health/completion colors independent of goal identity.
