---
name: Goal priority and spotlight
description: Keep overall goal importance separate from the user's current focus.
---

Priority means overall importance; spotlight means current focus. Keep them independent, with a recommendation of three or fewer spotlight goals but no hard limit.

**Why:** The user approved this distinction so important goals can remain visible without turning personal planning into project management.

**How to apply:** Spotlight changes visibility and placement only, not deadlines, measured progress, task priorities, or schedules. Only active goals occupy the focus section. Preserve a paused goal's spotlight preference so resuming it restores its focus placement; inactive goals must not be presented as currently in focus. Keep individual goal colors rather than introducing a competing spotlight color.
