---
name: Missed work decisions
description: Explicit decisions for missed tasks and sessions, and skip versus completion boundaries.
---

Missed tasks and sessions must remain visible on Overview and Planner until the user decides to replan or skip them.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked the app to prompt a decision about missed work rather than silently leave it behind.

**How to apply:** Preserve explicit choices. Moving an item to another day resolves the missed-work prompt; do not repeatedly flag the same old deadline after an intentional replan.

Skipping means the occurrence did not happen, not that the task was finished or time was worked. Keep its history and continue recurring work without generating a backlog of old occurrences. Include today's occurrence when it is due; do not silently skip today's work while resolving an older missed occurrence.

**Why:** The user wants to ignore a missed occurrence and proceed to the next session, without false goal progress or extra catch-up work.

**How to apply:** Keep skip history separate from actual results, task completion, and client-billable time. Recurring successors retain their cadence.
